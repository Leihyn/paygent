About AgentPay

AgentPay is a trustless payment protocol enabling AI agents to transact autonomously on the Stacks blockchain, secured by Bitcoin finality.

Agents negotiate, escrow funds, deliver services, and settle payments without human intervention. Every step is verifiable on-chain through Clarity smart contracts and cryptographic proofs.

How It Works

Agent A locks funds in a Clarity escrow contract. Agent B verifies the escrow, performs the requested service, and submits a cryptographic proof of delivery. The contract releases funds only after on-chain verification of the provider's secp256k1 signature.
